# Business Questions

The goal of this project is to analyze customer behavior using cohort and retention analysis and identify patterns in repeat purchases and customer churn.

## Key Business Questions

1. How many new customers were acquired each month?
2. How can customers be grouped into monthly cohorts based on their first purchase?
3. What percentage of customers returned in the months following their first purchase?
4. How does customer retention change over time for each cohort?
5. Which customer cohorts have the highest repeat purchase rate?
6. How much revenue does each cohort generate over time?
7. What is the average time between the first and second purchase?
8. At which stage does the largest decline in customer retention occur?

## Planned Metrics

- New Customers
- Cohort Size
- Retained Customers
- Monthly Retention Rate
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Revenue by Cohort
- Average Time to Second Purchase
